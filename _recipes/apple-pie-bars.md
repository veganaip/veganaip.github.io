Cozy, cinnamon-spiced apple filling over a buttery tigernut crust - these bars capture everything you love about apple pie.

### Make the Crust

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a 9x9 inch baking pan with parchment paper.

2. In a bowl, combine cassava flour, tigernut flour, and salt.

3. Cut in cold coconut oil using a fork or pastry cutter until mixture resembles coarse crumbs.

4. Drizzle in maple syrup and mix until dough comes together.

5. Press 2/3 of the crust mixture into the bottom of the prepared pan firmly and evenly. Reserve remaining 1/3 for topping.

6. Bake crust for 10 minutes while preparing filling.

### Make the Filling

7. Peel and thinly slice apples. Place in a large bowl.

8. Add maple syrup, cinnamon, ginger, arrowroot, and lemon juice. Toss to coat evenly.

9. Arrange apple slices over pre-baked crust in overlapping layers.

10. Crumble reserved crust mixture over the top.

### Bake and Serve

11. Bake for 35-40 minutes until apples are tender and topping is golden.

12. Let cool for at least 20 minutes before cutting into bars.

### Tips
- Use firm, tart apples like Granny Smith for best results
- For softer apples like Honeycrisp, reduce baking time by 5 minutes
- Serve warm with coconut cream for an extra treat
- These reheat well in a 300°F oven for 10 minutes
