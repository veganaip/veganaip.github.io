Creamy, tropical, and utterly satisfying - this pudding brings island flavors to your table.

### Instructions

1. In a medium saucepan, combine tapioca pearls with 2 cups of water. Let soak for 30 minutes (or follow package directions for quick-cook tapioca).

2. Bring tapioca and soaking water to a boil over medium heat, stirring frequently to prevent sticking.

3. Reduce heat and simmer for 10-12 minutes, stirring often, until tapioca pearls are mostly translucent.

4. Stir in coconut milk, maple syrup, and salt. Continue cooking for 5-8 minutes, stirring frequently, until mixture thickens.

5. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla, lime juice, and half the lime zest.

6. In a blender, puree 1.5 cups of the mango until smooth. Dice remaining mango for topping.

7. Fold mango puree into the tapioca mixture, creating swirls of orange throughout.

8. Serve warm or transfer to serving dishes and refrigerate for at least 2 hours to chill.

9. Top with diced mango, toasted coconut flakes, and remaining lime zest before serving.

### Tips
- Small pearl tapioca creates the classic pudding texture; large pearls make it more like boba
- Pudding will thicken as it cools - if too thick, stir in a splash of coconut milk
- For a mousse-like texture, blend the finished pudding before chilling
- Try with other tropical fruits: papaya, passion fruit, or pineapple
- Toast coconut flakes in a dry pan over medium heat for 2-3 minutes
