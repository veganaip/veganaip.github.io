A fast, flavorful stir-fry with tender vegetables over fluffy cauliflower rice - ready in 30 minutes.

### Prepare the Cauliflower Rice

1. Cut cauliflower into florets. Pulse in a food processor until rice-sized. Set aside.

2. Prep all vegetables before you start cooking - stir-frying goes quickly.

### Stir-Fry the Vegetables

3. Heat 1 tablespoon coconut oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat.

4. Add onion and carrots. Stir-fry for 3 minutes.

5. Add garlic, ginger, and zucchini. Stir-fry for 2 minutes.

6. Add bok choy and stir-fry for 2 minutes until just wilted.

7. Push vegetables to the side of the pan.

### Cook the Cauliflower Rice

8. Add remaining 1 tablespoon coconut oil to the empty side of the pan.

9. Add cauliflower rice and spread into an even layer.

10. Let it cook undisturbed for 2 minutes, then stir.

11. Cook for another 2-3 minutes until slightly golden.

### Combine and Serve

12. Mix vegetables and cauliflower rice together.

13. Add coconut aminos and salt. Toss to combine.

14. Top with sliced green onions and serve immediately.

### Tips
- Buy pre-riced cauliflower to save time
- Use any AIP-compliant vegetables you have on hand
- Add shredded jackfruit for a heartier meal
- Leftovers keep for 3 days but are best eaten fresh
